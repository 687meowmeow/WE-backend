Frequently Asked Questions About Wallpaper Engine for Android

Which devices are supported?

Wallpaper Engine supports all phones and tablets running Android 10 or later.

Can I set a wallpaper on the Android lock screen?

This depends on your device. Some Android manufacturers do not allow live wallpapers on the lock screen. If your device supports them, the option to set a lock screen wallpaper appears automatically.

Does Wallpaper Engine on Android affect battery life?

The impact on battery life is minimal. Wallpaper Engine runs only while the wallpaper is visible. In the app settings, you can choose a quality level and set an FPS limit. You can also pause wallpapers automatically when power saving mode is on, helping you balance wallpaper quality and battery life throughout the day.

How do I add more wallpapers to the app?

Due to technical limitations, Wallpaper Engine on Android cannot directly access the Steam Workshop to download new wallpapers. However, you can transfer your wallpaper collection wirelessly onto your phone via Wallpaper Engine on your computer.

The app itself works always the same way and networking is a fairly standardized topic. We tested the networking feature extensively on various networks under different conditions and it works reliably unless a firewall or VPN blocks or redirects network traffic - which is fine because that's what these apps are literally programmed to do.

Incidentally you seem to be using both so if you continue to have connection issues, the most sensible explanation for this is that one of your many networking apps is interfering with your local network traffic. The next time you run into any issues, please double-check that any firewall app ignores all traffic from ui32.exe and that the network ports 7884 (UDP) and 7889 (TCP) are not being blocked by any firewall, including any firewall built into your network router.

If you have not already, please double-check our mobile pairing problem solving guide:

https://help.wallpaperengine.io/en/mobile/pairing-fixes.html
